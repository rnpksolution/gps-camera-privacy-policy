GPS CAMERA HARYANA – Account Deletion

This page explains how users of GPS CAMERA HARYANA, developed by RNPK, can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your GPS CAMERA HARYANA account, send an email to our support address from the email address associated with your account, if available.

Please include your registered mobile number and clearly state that you want your GPS CAMERA HARYANA account deleted.

Email: rnpk.solution@gmail.com

Request Account Deletion

Information to Include in Your Request

  • Your registered mobile number.
  • Your registered email address, if applicable.
  • Your name, if it is associated with your account.
  • A clear statement requesting account deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted

After verifying the deletion request, we will delete the account and personal information associated with the account that is maintained by GPS CAMERA HARYANA, subject to applicable legal, security, regulatory, or fraud-prevention requirements.

Depending on the services used by the app, this may include account information, registered mobile number, profile information, and other account-associated personal data maintained by us.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained when required or permitted by applicable law, for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Any information that must be retained will be kept only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose and will be deleted or securely disposed of when it is no longer required.

How the Request Is Processed

When we receive an account deletion request, we may verify the requester's identity or account ownership before processing the request.

Once the request is verified, the applicable account and associated personal data will be deleted in accordance with our data retention practices and applicable requirements.

Partial Data Deletion

If you want specific personal data removed without deleting your entire account, you may contact us at the email address below. We will review the request and determine whether the requested data can be deleted while maintaining the account.
